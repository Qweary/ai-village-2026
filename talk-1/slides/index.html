THE RAILS
four of them. all four are in the artifacts you can open.
1
No offensive package without a separate role whose only job is verifying authorization and clearing each phase. That role gets built first, before the agents that do the work. Build the gate before the gun.
2
Everything it generates points at synthetic address space. An offline lab or a fictional range. The scenario says so in front of the model, in the prompt.
3
It will not argue with a refusal. There was a long framing block in front of these models once, the kind that pre-answers every objection. It got deleted. Pre-answering objections is the literal shape of a jailbreak. Short factual grounding, or nothing.
4
The improvement loop will not push a rewritten prompt into a role it does not match. It checks, and it refuses.

collapse · what i would instrument instead
0:46
FIVE THINGS. NONE OF THEM ARE HARD.
HOLDOUT
Keep at least one exercise the loop never gets to tune against, and score every rewrite on it too. The score on the exercise you tuned on is training loss. Only the holdout gets to say the word improved.
REGRESSION RATE
Count the fraction of rounds that come back worse. A scorer that has never once returned a negative result has not been shown to work. Alarm on the absence.
LINEAGE
Log which ancestor every surviving prompt came from. When every survivor traces back to one, you have one agent wearing several name tags. Trivially measurable, and nobody measures it.
NOVELTY
Count the distinct techniques attempted per round. When that falls, the fight is narrowing, and a narrowing fight pushes the score up for a reason that has nothing to do with anybody getting better.
NOISE FLOOR
Score the same fixed exercise several times without changing a character, and look at the spread. That distance is your noise floor. An improvement smaller than it is not a result yet. Do this one first.
none of that is in the architecture by default. you have to go and add it.
